
## 📖 About the Project

** 🐝 Budget Bee** is a modern expense tracking web application that helps users manage their income and expenses efficiently. It provides clear insights into spending habits through interactive dashboards and smart categorization.

Designed especially for students and individuals, Budget Bee simplifies financial tracking and promotes better money management.

---

## ✨ Features

* 💰 **Income & Expense Tracking**
  Easily add, edit, and delete transactions

* 📊 **Visual Dashboard**
  Analyze spending using charts and graphs

* 🗂️ **Category-Based Management**
  Organize expenses (Food, Travel, Shopping, etc.)

* 🔍 **Search & Filter**
  Quickly find transactions by name or category

* 📅 **Daily & Monthly Tracking**
  Monitor spending patterns over time

* ⚠️ **Budget Limit Alerts**
  Get notified when you exceed your budget

* 🤖 **AI Spending Suggestions (Upcoming)**
  Smart insights to reduce unnecessary expenses
